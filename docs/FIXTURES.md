# Fixtures

All fixtures are original work of this repository (MIT). They are copied into
every image under `/fixtures` (`print-fixture <path>`), and
`scripts/validate-fixtures.py` checks them against [schemas/](../schemas)
and the cross-field rules of the contract.

## Frames (`fixtures/frames/*.jsonl`)

JSON Lines of [frame documents](CONTAINER_CONTRACT.md#frame-document); `#`
lines are comments.

| File | Frames | Covers |
|------|--------|--------|
| `standard.jsonl` | 27 | 11-bit identifiers 0x000, 0x123, 0x7FF, every classical length 0–8 |
| `extended.jsonl` | 36 | 29-bit identifiers 0, 0x123 (same number as a standard one), 0x12345678, 0x1FFFFFFF, lengths 0–8 |
| `remote.jsonl` | 8 | Remote frames with requested lengths 0, 1, 4, 8, 11- and 29-bit |
| `fd.jsonl` | 64 | CAN FD, every legal length (0–8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 48, 64), with and without BRS, 11- and 29-bit |

| `mixed.jsonl` | 42 | Classical, remote and FD frames interleaved, sent with `--fd`: classical frames must stay classical |
| `error.jsonl` | 5 | Error frames (classes controller, bus-off, lost arbitration, protocol violation, error counters) |
| `burst.jsonl` | 2000 | Classical frames; bytes 0–3 a big-endian sequence number for order and loss checks |

## KCD (`fixtures/kcd/`)

`basic.kcd`: a Kayak network definition with Intel and big-endian signals,
scaling, labels, a 29-bit message and multiplexing, with
`basic.frames.jsonl`. The self-test converts it with canmatrix and compares
cantools' decoding of the converted DBC with canmatrix's decoding of the KCD.

## ARXML (`fixtures/arxml/`)

AUTOSAR 4 system descriptions (schema namespace `http://autosar.org/schema/r4.0`),
original work, generated by `scripts/gen-arxml-fixtures.py` together with
their `.frames.jsonl` (decode) and `.messages.jsonl` (encode) vectors. The
self-test decodes and encodes them with cantools and canmatrix directly, and
decodes them with cantools after canmatrix converts them to DBC.

| File | Covers |
|------|--------|
| `basic.arxml` | `CAN-CLUSTER` / `CAN-FRAME-TRIGGERING` with `STANDARD` and `EXTENDED` addressing, `CAN-FRAME`, `PDU-TO-FRAME-MAPPING`, `I-SIGNAL-I-PDU`, `I-SIGNAL-TO-I-PDU-MAPPING` with `MOST-SIGNIFICANT-BYTE-LAST` (Intel) and `-FIRST` (Motorola, including a 32-bit one), `I-SIGNAL` → `SYSTEM-SIGNAL` → `COMPU-METHOD` of categories `LINEAR`, `TEXTTABLE`, `SCALE_LINEAR_AND_TEXTTABLE` and `IDENTICAL`, signed signals through `SW-BASE-TYPE` with `BASE-TYPE-ENCODING` `2C`, `INIT-VALUE`, a `UNIT` |
| `fd.arxml` | CAN FD frames (`CAN-FRAME-RX-BEHAVIOR`/`CAN-FRAME-TX-BEHAVIOR` `CAN-FD`) of 64 bytes (standard) and 12 bytes (extended), signals at the end of the payload, a signed 32-bit signal |
| `pdu-offset.arxml` | A PDU mapped at byte 2 of its frame (`PDU-TO-FRAME-MAPPING` `START-POSITION` 16) |

What both oracles (cantools 45.1.0, canmatrix 1.3.3) read the same way:

* `START-POSITION` of an `I-SIGNAL-TO-I-PDU-MAPPING` is the DBC start bit, in
  both byte orders (a 12-bit `MOST-SIGNIFICANT-BYTE-FIRST` signal at 20 is
  `20|12@0+`).
* `COMPU-NUMERATOR` `<V>offset</V><V>factor</V>` over a denominator of 1.
* `TEXTTABLE` entries (equal lower and upper limit, `COMPU-CONST`/`VT`) are
  value descriptions; in `SCALE_LINEAR_AND_TEXTTABLE` the scale with
  `COMPU-RATIONAL-COEFFS` gives factor and offset.
* `BASE-TYPE-ENCODING` `2C` makes a signal signed.
* `CAN-FD` frame behaviour makes a CAN FD frame.
* `INIT-VALUE` is the raw initial value.

Where they fall short, so a consumer cannot use them as oracles:

* **PDU offset.** Neither applies the `START-POSITION` of a
  `PDU-TO-FRAME-MAPPING`: the signals of a PDU at frame byte 2 are decoded
  as if it started at byte 0. AUTOSAR places them after the offset. The
  vectors of `pdu-offset.arxml` record the oracles' (offset-less)
  behaviour; the self-test's `arxml-pdu-offset` scenario reports it as
  `known` (tests/known-differences.tsv).
* **Limits.** `LOWER-LIMIT`/`UPPER-LIMIT` of a compu scale are internal
  (raw) values in AUTOSAR; cantools converts them to physical limits,
  canmatrix computes limits from the signal width instead. The fixtures put
  no limits on linear scales.
* **ECUs.** Neither reads senders and receivers from these minimal files
  (they need `ECU-INSTANCE` with frame ports and connectors); not covered.
* **Multiplexed PDUs** (`MULTIPLEXED-I-PDU`) and secured PDUs are not
  covered by the fixtures.

## Logs (`fixtures/logs/`)

`frames.jsonl` (the frames of `fixtures/frames/mixed`, `fd` and `remote`,
1 ms apart) written by python-can 4.6.1 as `frames.asc`, `frames.blf` and
`frames.trc`, and `<format>.read.jsonl`, python-can's reading of each file,
losses included. Regenerate with `scripts/gen-log-fixtures.sh`; the
self-test (suite `logs`) checks that each file reads back as committed. The
losses per format are in [LOG_FORMATS.md](LOG_FORMATS.md).
